# CoolWheels — Validation Survey
Paste these into Google Forms, Tally, or Typeform. Keep it under 2 minutes to complete.

1. Do you use a wheelchair or other mobility aid? (Yes / No / Prefer not to say)
2. During hot weather, have you ever had trouble finding shade or a place to rest
   while out in public? (Yes / No / Sometimes)
3. Has heat ever affected your wheelchair or mobility aid (e.g. battery draining
   faster, controls becoming hot to use)? (Yes / No / Not applicable)
4. How often is it hard to find an *accessible* shaded spot (not just shade, but
   one you can actually reach)? (Never / Rarely / Sometimes / Often)
5. Would a public cooling pod — shade, misting, and charging for your mobility
   aid — be useful to you? (Yes, very / Somewhat / Not really)
6. Would you use a pod that also had space to charge your phone or work for a
   short time? (Yes / No / Depends on location)
7. What would make you trust and actually use a station like this?
   (Open text)
8. Anything else you'd want us to know before we build a pilot?
   (Open text)

## Notes for the team
- Keep questions 1–6 as single-choice/multiple-choice for fast quantitative results.
- Questions 7–8 give qualitative insight for the coach meeting and for refining
  the Idea Card.
- Aim to reach disability services offices, mobility-aid user groups, or campus
  accessibility contacts rather than a general audience — the sample needs to
  match the target user.
