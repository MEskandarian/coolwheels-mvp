# Course framework

## Course
EURECA-PRO Deep Dive Intensive Course in Innovation and Entrepreneurship (I&E DDIC 2026), 14 Sept to 9 Oct 2026, hosted via Mittweida University of Applied Sciences. Challenge: "Cooling Europe", entrepreneurial solutions for heat-safe public spaces. Teams of up to 6 for the certificate.

Design challenge given to all groups: How can we redesign or reimagine public spaces in European cities and communities to make them more heat-safe, inclusive and climate-resilient?

## Sessions
1. Design Thinking workshop (SAXEED workbook, Andre Uhlmann)
2. Walking through a Living Lab (not in exam scope)
3. Lean Startup and Minimum Viable Product (Dr. Remo Taferner)
4. Pitching Perfectly seminar (Prof. Knauer)
5. Project coaching (30 min online per group, booked via Moodle, 250 to 300 word summary posted to the "Ask your Project Coach" forum beforehand)
6. Final pitch 2026-10-08, peer review, exam on Moodle (multiple choice, 120 min, 20% of course grade, pass at 50%)

## Design Thinking phases used (SAXEED workbook)
- Phase 1 Understand: Semantic Analysis (problem, key terms, define meaning), Charette Chart (users, problems)
- Phase 2 Observe: interviews. Structure: introduction, warm-up and context, deep dive and peak emotions, reflect and wrap-up
- Phase 3 Definition: storytelling (three key insights), problem statement "(User) needs (need) because / but / although / surprisingly (insight)"
- Phase 4 Ideation: Crazy Five, partner feedback, Matrix analysis (easy/hard to implement vs low/high benefit for the user), Idea Card (slogan, target group, problem, offer, look, money, radicalness)
