# Business model and financials

Source: Google Sheet "CoolWheels_Startup_Unit_Economics". All values are illustrative modelling assumptions, not validated data. Utilization is unknown until the pilot.

## Core assumptions
Installed pilot pod cost EUR 18,000. Annual service contract EUR 2,500 per pod. Plus sponsorship 20% of install cost. Economic validation EUR 5,000. Pilot ask EUR 23,000. Pilot 3 months. Max session 15 min. 1 user per pod.
Editable modelling assumption: standard pod sale price EUR 22,000.

## Revenue streams
Municipal pod sale and installation, annual service contract, CSR sponsorship on Plus pods, pilot funding by angel investors or crowdfunding, possible minimal insurance-covered user fee.

## Break-even
Weighted upfront contribution per new pod EUR 7,010. Weighted annual service contribution per active pod EUR 1,098. Y1 fixed plus validation costs EUR 17,000. New pods to cover Y1 costs: 2.4. Service-only active pods to cover Y1 fixed cost: 10.9.

## Weak points to defend (R1 to R3)
- R1: the 5-year pod counts (1, 3, 10, 25, 50) have no demand evidence yet.
- R2: Cost Build-Up and Unit Economics do not match. Standard: build-up totals EUR 16,000 (direct 11,500, installation 2,500, compliance 2,000) vs COGS EUR 17,000 (direct 12,500). Plus: build-up EUR 19,200 vs COGS EUR 19,500. Align before quoting either.
- R3: daily utilization is unknown with 1 user per pod and 15 min sessions, which caps throughput.
